## Merchant / AI feed eligibility — no integration built

**Google Shopping:** Iraq is absent from Google's current [supported target countries](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12472394?hl=en). Do not select another country to bypass that limitation or convert IQD prices to invented USD amounts. ISO currency in ordinary Product schema is a different question from Merchant Center target-market eligibility. [Website requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150244?hl=en) also require a supported purchase experience and clear business/returns information. WhatsApp enquiry and Wayl seller-subscription payments do not establish that customer checkout requirement. Organic Google Search/Images discovery can still be pursued.

**OpenAI:** [Feed onboarding](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/guides/get-started) is restricted to approved partners. The current [file schema](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products) defaults to the US; additional markets/currencies require OpenAI confirmation. Iraq/IQD/Sorani and Bazaro's multi-seller WhatsApp model are **unconfirmed**, not enabled. Required fields include real brand, seller, description, stable product URL, image, price and availability (explicit unknown is supported in that format). Bazaro lacks reliable structured brand/inventory and five inspected descriptions. No brand inference or in-stock claim is permissible. Do not build/submit a feed or accept merchant terms until partner/market approval and truthful field coverage are resolved. ChatGPT Search crawl access is separate.
